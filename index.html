Work

Built at the workshop in North Boambee Valley or welded on site: trays, camper boxes, trailers, boat work and gates.

What we build

  • Trays & canopies

    Aluminium trays and canopies for utes and trucks, with drawers, shelving and under-tray toolboxes.

  • Camper boxes

    Camper boxes for trucks, with side windows and checker-plate skirts.

  • Trailers

    Aluminium car trailers with checker-plate toolboxes, and twin jet-ski trailers.

  • Boats & boat modifications

    Aluminium boat work, such as welded marine tanks, and modifications to boats you already own.

  • Marine & stainless

    Solar arches, outboard brackets and rails, bent and welded from stainless steel.

  • On-site welding

    Welded where the job is, such as steel cattle yards with a shade roof.

  • Gates & fencing

    Steel sliding gates with timber infill, and aluminium slat fences and gates.

  • One-off parts

    Custom aluminium airboxes, stainless IBC trays and mounts made as one-offs.

The workshop

Ruari works in aluminium, stainless and mild steel out of his workshop on Englands Rd, North Boambee Valley. He takes on everything from simple work to big: a toolbox under a tray, a solar arch for a yacht, a set of cattle yards.

When a job can’t come to the workshop, he takes the welder to it.
